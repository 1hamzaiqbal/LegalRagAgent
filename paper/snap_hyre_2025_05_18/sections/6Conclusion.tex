\section{What Snap-HyRE Does and Does Not Fix}\label{sec:conclusion}

\hyre{} is a fixed generated-query intervention, not a universal replacement
for raw retrieval. It is strongest on BarExamQA, where draft-guided legal
passages improve both Hit@5 and final answer accuracy. HousingQA marks the
boundary: once retrieval is scoped to the correct state, raw questions can
already be strong search queries.

The failures are stage-specific: a draft can choose the wrong frame, a passage
can drop anchors, and an answer model can misuse good evidence. Legal RAG
papers should report retrieval exposure, answer use, and corpus-interface
assumptions. \hyre{} helps when a question must be rewritten into authority
language before retrieval begins.
