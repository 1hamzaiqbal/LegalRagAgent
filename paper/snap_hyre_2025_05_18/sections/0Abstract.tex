\begin{abstract}
Legal RAG often fails before the answer model sees useful law: the question is
a poor search query. We study \hyre{}, a fixed two-call method that first
writes a private draft answer and a separate legal search passage, retrieves
with only that passage, and then answers from retrieved evidence. On
BarExamQA, \hyre{} improves over raw-question RAG for all three model sizes,
with gains up to +5.1 percentage points and consistent Hit@5 gains. HousingQA
shows the boundary condition: jurisdiction filtering raises raw Hit@5 from
2.8\% to 36.9\%, after which raw state-filtered retrieval is a strong
baseline. The results support a narrow claim: draft-guided legal search
passages help in weak-query regimes, and legal RAG papers should report answer
accuracy together with whether the right legal evidence was retrieved.
\end{abstract}
