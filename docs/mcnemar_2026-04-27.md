# McNemar confirmation: multi_hyde_diverse vs rag_simple

Computed with `scripts/compute_mcnemar.py`, paired on `idx`, exact two-sided
McNemar binomial test, and 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples for the accuracy
difference CI. Gemma N=200 tags were validated through `logs/experiments.jsonl`;
the detail rows do not carry inline `tag` fields.

| Comparison | n_paired | acc_baseline | acc_treatment | delta_pp | b | c | McNemar p | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gemma 27B N=200: multi_hyde_diverse vs rag_simple | 200 | 28.5% | 31.0% | +2.5 | 30 | 25 | 0.5901 | [-5.0, +9.5] pp |
| Llama 70b N=100: multi_hyde_diverse vs rag_simple | 100 | 21.0% | 33.0% | +12.0 | 18 | 6 | 0.0227 | [+3.0, +21.0] pp |

Interpretation: Gemma 27B N=200 does not hit p<0.05; with p=0.5901 it is a
null confirmation under the requested bins, not even a 0.05<p<0.15 trend. The
prior Gemma N=100 effect was +8.0pp (22/100 to 30/100, McNemar p about 0.134);
the N=200 power-up shrank to +2.5pp (57/200 to 62/200), a 5.5pp absolute
shrink. The mhd cross-family lift therefore does not survive Tier 2 N=200
confirmation on Gemma, even though the requested Llama 70b N=100 pair remains
significant.

- Multi-hop method (mhd) lifts MuSiQue, NOT BarExam
