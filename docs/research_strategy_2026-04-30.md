# Research Strategy - 2026-04-30

This note is a reset after the 2026-04-28 bottleneck-taxonomy pivot. It is
meant to decide what this project can honestly say, what it should stop
trying to say, and which infra gaps block a more thorough investigation.

## Current Verdict

Do not lead with `snap_hyde_2call` as method novelty. The literature has too
many close neighbors: HyDE, Query2Doc, FLARE, Self-RAG, Adaptive-RAG, DRAGIN,
Iter-RetGen, CoRAG, and Speculative RAG all cover nearby ground. The stronger
project is not "we invented a new RAG recipe." It is:

> RAG should be treated as an intervention whose value depends on the dominant
> failure mode of the task: missing information, retrieval coverage,
> evidence-utilization/composition, or answer-option disambiguation.

That framing is more grounded and more useful than another method leaderboard.
It also makes the negative results valuable instead of embarrassing.

is catastrophic on MuSiQue and flat on BarExam; that difference predicts whether
retrieval-heavy interventions are worth trying."

## Literature Boundary

Primary-source checks:

- HyDE generates a hypothetical document for zero-shot dense retrieval, then
  grounds it by retrieving similar real documents. This covers the pseudo-doc
  retrieval primitive. Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.99/
- Query2Doc generates pseudo-documents and expands the original query for sparse
  and dense retrieval. Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.585/
- FLARE actively decides when and what to retrieve during generation by using a
  tentative future sentence as a query. Source:
  https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.495/
- Self-RAG trains reflection tokens for retrieval-on-demand and critique. Source:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11511
- Adaptive-RAG routes between no retrieval, single-step retrieval, and multi-step
  retrieval based on question complexity. Source:
  https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.389/
- DRAGIN decides when and what to retrieve from real-time information needs
  during generation. Source: https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.702/
- Iter-RetGen and CoRAG both frame multi-hop QA as iterative
  retrieval-generation. Sources: https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.620/
  and https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14342
- Speculative RAG drafts from retrieved subsets and verifies with a larger model.
  Source: https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/2ea06b52f613716e67458f5ab3fb7558-Abstract-Conference.html
- Know Your RAG and GRADE show that dataset/question structure matters for RAG
  evaluation, but they are taxonomies/benchmarks rather than a direct
  task-bottleneck intervention story. Sources:
  https://aclanthology.org/2025.coling-industry.4/ and
  https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.236/
- LegalBench-RAG and A Reasoning-Focused Legal Retrieval Benchmark establish
  that legal RAG needs retrieval-specific evaluation and realistic legal QA.
  Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10343 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03970
- Legal RAG Bench is especially important new context: it uses a factorial
  retrieval-model x generation-model design and a hierarchical error
  decomposition, concluding that retrieval often sets the ceiling in its legal
  long-form benchmark. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01710
- Vaddi 2026 is the closest local-neighborhood result for "task-dependent legal
  retrieval": small/MoE models, architecture effects, and task-dependent outcomes
  in legal document reasoning. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25944

Implication: the contribution has to be diagnostic and mechanistic. A reviewer
can compress the method itself to "FLARE/HyDE-ish." They cannot as easily
dismiss a disciplined error-attribution framework that explains why the same
retrieval intervention helps one benchmark, is neutral on another, and hurts a
third.

## Better Narrative

The project should be framed as a study of RAG intervention design:

1. Start from the empirical puzzle: RAG helps dramatically in some QA settings,
   barely moves others, and sometimes hurts even when evidence is available.
2. Argue that the problem is underspecified because "RAG" bundles several
   separable interventions: retrieving missing facts, choosing retrieval depth,
   converting questions into retrievable pseudo-documents, helping the model
   compose evidence, and anchoring answer-option decisions.
3. Introduce a small diagnostic suite: no-context, retrieved-context at k=1/5,
   single gold-passage, multi-query/HyDE variants, and snap/answer-anchored
   controls.
4. Use the diagnostics to label task regimes:
   - retrieval-depth limited
   - retrieval-query limited
   - evidence-utilization limited
   - answer-option disambiguation limited
   - retriever-quality limited
5. Show that method choice follows the regime, not the method name. This turns
   the nulls into evidence.

Possible paper thesis:

> RAG failures are not one problem. Across multi-hop QA and legal reasoning
> benchmarks, the same retrieval intervention can be helpful, irrelevant, or
> harmful depending on whether the binding constraint is recall, composition,
> or answer-option disambiguation. We propose a diagnostic matrix for assigning
> benchmarks to bottleneck regimes and use it to predict when retrieval-heavy
> methods should be replaced by reasoning or anchoring controls.

## What Not To Claim

- Do not claim `snap_hyde_2call` is fundamentally novel. It is a useful probe
  and a cost-efficient implementation, not the intellectual center.
- Do not claim model-agnostic lift on MuSiQue. Gemma 27B mhd at N=200 is null.
- Do not call `golden_passage` an oracle ceiling on legal MC. It is a
  single-passage context control.
- Do not frame CaseHOLD/SCALR as failures to hide. They are important evidence
  that option-disambiguation benchmarks do not automatically benefit from
  retrieval.
- Do not write the story around one N=200 significant row. Use N=200 as a
  diagnostic, then ask whether the regime repeats.

## Recommended Next Experiments

   `llm_only`, `rag_simple` k=1/5/10, `golden_passage`, `rag_multi_query`,
   `multi_hyde_diverse`, `iter_hyde`, `snap_only_in_final`, `rag_snap_hyde_2call`.
   Datasets: MuSiQue, BarExam, HousingQA, LegalBench-SCALR, CaseHOLD.
2. Add Legal RAG Bench before FRAMES if the goal is legal motivation. It is
   small, recent, has corpus plus relevant passage IDs, and matches the
   retrieval-vs-reasoning decomposition this project now needs.
3. Add FRAMES after Legal RAG Bench if the goal is multi-hop generalization.
   It is stronger taxonomically than another MuSiQue N=200 row, but requires a
   corpus decision and an LLM judge.
4. Add MLEB-SCALR retrieval-only scoring as a retriever calibration side quest,
   not as a QA headline.
5. Use the new evidence matrix after every run. If a row cannot survive direct
   log recomputation in that matrix, it should not become prose.

## Paper Direction

The honest paper is a negative-and-diagnostic paper with one positive method
probe:

- Positive result: retrieval-sensitive multi-hop can benefit from
  snap/pseudo-doc or diverse query formulation under Llama 70b.
- Negative result: legal MC and holding-disambiguation tasks often do not
  benefit from extra retrieval, even when RAG methods are more expensive.
- Mechanistic result: top-k sensitivity and gold/single-passage controls reveal
  whether the system lacks facts, fails to retrieve them, fails to use them, or
  is already solving an option-disambiguation task from parametric knowledge.
- Tooling contribution: a reproducible evaluation matrix that forces method
  claims to be tied to bottleneck diagnostics.

This is less flashy than "new RAG architecture wins," but it is more defensible
and probably more useful.
