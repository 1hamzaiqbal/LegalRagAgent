# SkillMOO

## TL;DR

SkillMOO optimizes bundles of external agent skills over task success, cost,
and runtime on software-engineering tasks.

## Bearing

This closes “optimize skills for accuracy and cost” as a novelty claim.
External skill optimization can be a later baseline, but the proposed primary
question concerns a smaller model's internal conditional policy.
