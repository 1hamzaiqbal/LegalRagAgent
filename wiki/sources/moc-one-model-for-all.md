# One Model for All

## TL;DR

This work trains one policy conditioned on user objective preferences and
evaluates generalization to unseen preferences.

## Bearing

It closes the generic claim that a single model can span a controllable
multi-objective frontier or interpolate to unseen tradeoff weights. Our
candidate must be specifically about cross-scale preservation of agent action
switches under paired same-task price interventions, with a direct conditioned
RL baseline.
