# ClawTrace and CostCraft

## TL;DR

ClawTrace records per-step model/tool/subagent cost, tokens, latency, and
redundancy in structured TraceCards. CostCraft uses those signals to preserve,
prune, or repair external skill patches.

## Bearing

This is the closest collision with “cost-aware skill distillation.” That phrase
cannot be our contribution. CostCraft changes an external text skill; the
candidate in [[01-research-question-and-novelty]] asks whether a smaller
weight-space policy preserves a teacher's counterfactual price response.
