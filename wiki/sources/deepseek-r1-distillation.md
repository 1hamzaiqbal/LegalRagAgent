# DeepSeek-R1 distillation

## TL;DR

DeepSeek uses 800k samples generated by R1 to SFT several smaller Qwen/Llama
base models. The distilled models substantially improve on their original
instruction-tuned or RL-only counterparts and beat tested closed models on
some reasoning benchmarks.

## Evidence

- The distilled students use SFT only in this paper; the authors leave
  post-distillation RL to future work.
- Table 15 reports the 1.5B student above GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on AIME
  and MATH-500, but below them on GPQA and coding.
- The 32B distilled model beats a 32B model trained for more than 10k RL steps
  across the reported reasoning benchmarks.
- The paper notes that R1 already varies its token use by problem difficulty
  but still overthinks easy items.

## Bearing on our work

This is strong feasibility evidence for a small specialist exceeding frontier
generalists on narrow tasks. It does not train or evaluate a user-controllable
accuracy–cost frontier. A new result must pre-register the held-out task and
compare matched costs and sampling protocols, rather than choose benchmarks
where the distilled specialist is known to excel.
