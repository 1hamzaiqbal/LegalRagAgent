# Reasoning Gym

## TL;DR

Reasoning Gym provides more than 100 procedural reasoning generators and
algorithmic verifiers across algebra, arithmetic, computation, graphs, logic,
and games. Difficulty is configurable and data can be generated from fresh
seeds.

## Bearing

It is the proposed core substrate for [[04-benchmark-audit-and-eda]] because it
avoids a small static legal benchmark and supports paired same-task price
interventions with deterministic scoring. Native rewards are heterogeneous and
must be audited rather than treated as uniformly binary.
