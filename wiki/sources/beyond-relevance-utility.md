# Beyond Relevance: Utility-Centric Retrieval in the LLM Era

## TL;DR

This tutorial organizes the move from topical relevance to downstream utility,
including model-agnostic versus model-specific and context-independent versus
context-dependent approaches. Its existence is itself a novelty warning:
“utility rather than relevance” is now a recognized research area.

## Consequence for our work

We should use this taxonomy in related work and state the exact occupied cell:
model-specific, reader-conditioned, context/set-dependent, causal utility under
cost. A contribution needs a new estimator, benchmark protocol, or policy—not
only the vocabulary of helpfulness.
