# ConflictRAG

## TL;DR

ConflictRAG detects document–document and parametric–contextual conflicts,
ranks sources using multi-criteria credibility signals, and selectively
resolves or triggers additional search. It reports answer gains and lower cost
than always-on verification.

## Bearing on our work

The meeting idea “what if the model does not believe the evidence?” is occupied
at a generic RAG level. A legal version needs domain structure ConflictRAG does
not provide: binding versus persuasive authority, court hierarchy,
jurisdiction, date, negative treatment, and the distinction between factual
and normative conflict.
