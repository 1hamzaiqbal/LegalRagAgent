# CoRL budget controller

## TL;DR

CoRL trains a centralized controller to allocate among heterogeneous expert
LLMs under different budget levels, producing one cost-controllable system.

## Bearing

Budget-conditioned capability routing is not new. CoRL routes among models,
whereas the proposed pilot routes between internal reasoning and one priced
tool and asks whether that routing surface survives cross-scale distillation.
